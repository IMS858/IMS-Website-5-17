Analytics privacy supplement · September 28, 2026

Optional website analytics

This supplements the IMS privacy policy for the optional Google Analytics installation on imsmethod.com. Other services described in that policy, including hosting, Google Fonts, forms and booking, are separate from this choice.

Your choice comes first

Google Analytics is off until you choose Allow analytics. Declining or closing the prompt does not prevent browsing or booking. You can reopen Analytics preferences in the footer and choose Decline analytics at any time. Withdrawing consent disables this installation, clears its accessible analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop already-loaded analytics code. It does not retroactively erase data already received by Google.

We remember your choice in this browser's local storage for up to 180 days. If your browser blocks local storage, an allowance lasts for the current page only. This installation also stays off when the browser communicates Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

What the installation measures

After consent, Google Analytics receives selected public-page views and fixed chatbot interaction event codes, plus ordinary browser/device and request information needed to deliver its service. Analytics cookies can distinguish consenting browser visits; they do not make the data anonymous. IMS uses this reporting to understand and improve the public website.

This installation uses a declared list of public informational pages and Google tag G-9CYYFYT8ZY. Page names are predefined, page URLs sent by our configuration omit query strings and fragments, and configured referrers are reduced to an origin. Only a small list of approved campaign labels is eligible for tracking; URLs containing other query parameters or fragments are not measured by this installation.

Chatbot interaction counts, not transcripts

With analytics consent, eligible pages can report chat opens, submitted-message counts, rendered answers or errors, clicks on the three preset topics (assessment, pricing, recovery), and booking/contact/phone link clicks. These are interaction counts, not unique people, qualified leads, booked consultations, answered calls or revenue. At most 50 chatbot events per page are sent. Events before consent or before the analytics tag finishes loading are not collected later.

Only fixed event names and the minimized public-page context are sent to Google. No message text, AI response text, free-text topic classification, names, contact information or movement/health details are sent by this module. Chat messages still go to the AI service to answer the visitor under the main privacy policy; that processing is separate from optional analytics. The expanded choice asks for new consent rather than reusing the prior pageview-only preference.

What is excluded

The Google tag is not loaded by this installation on the booking, contact, practitioner enquiry or movement self-check pages, on unknown routes, on pages containing forms or embedded frames, on preview domains, or inside the separate private Coach OS application. Our analytics integration does not read form answers, chat messages, movement-check results, health information, names, email addresses, phone numbers or private client identifiers. It does not send a lead or purchase event merely because someone views or clicks a page.

Advertising storage, advertising user-data consent and personalized advertising consent remain denied. This installation does not enable Google signals, remarketing or enhanced lead conversions.

Storage and service provider

Google processes analytics requests under its service terms and privacy documentation. This installation's host-scoped analytics cookie names begin with ims_ga4 and are configured with a maximum age of 180 days. Your browser may retain them for less time. Report retention in the Google Analytics account is separate from cookie duration.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites. Browser content blockers may prevent analytics from working even after you allow it.

Contact delivery

The existing contact form continues through Web3Forms unless IMS explicitly enables direct email delivery. When enabled, the contact page identifies Resend as the email processor and IMS Coach OS as the private follow-up system. Message acceptance, inbox delivery, an automated acknowledgement and a personal coach reply are separate outcomes. These form fields are not sent to Google Analytics.
