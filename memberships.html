Terms

The practical details.

Cancellations and rescheduling

Twelve hours notice for a cancellation or reschedule. That protects your coach's schedule and keeps the slot available to other clients. Late cancellations and no-shows forfeit the session.

Can I pause a membership?

Yes — travel and life happen. Talk to Jason directly and we'll work it out rather than making you cancel and re-enroll.

What if I need to change tiers?

Move up or down at the start of any billing cycle. Most people start at two per week and adjust once they see what their schedule really supports.

Do unused sessions roll over?

Memberships are built around weekly consistency rather than banking sessions. If you know a light month is coming, a package may suit you better.
