Home / Practitioner space

Space for independent practitioners.

IMS has treatment and studio rooms available to practitioners who run their own practice. You keep your clients, your rates, and your business — we provide the room.

How it works

You run your practice. We provide the space.

Practitioners here operate entirely independently — own business, own clients, own scheduling, own rates. IMS is your landlord, not your employer or your booking agent.

Your business stays yours

You set your own rates, keep your own client relationships, and handle your own booking and payment. Nothing routes through IMS.

Established location

Scripps Ranch, Suite D, with parking directly outside and consistent weekday and Saturday foot traffic through the facility.

Equipped rooms

A Pilates studio with reformer, chair, and wall unit, plus a private treatment room suitable for massage or bodywork.

Available rooms

Two spaces, currently.

  • Pilates studio — reformer, combo chair, wall unit, mirror, mat space
  • Treatment room — private, quiet, suited to massage or manual therapy
  • Access to the shared lounge and restrooms for your clients
  • Flexible scheduling — regular weekly blocks or full-time

Rates depend on the room and the hours you need. Send a note below and Jason will get back to you with specifics.
