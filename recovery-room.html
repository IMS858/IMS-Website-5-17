Home / Recovery Room

A private room for the part most people skip.

Compression, infrared sauna, PEMF, percussion, and a vibration platform — in a room with a door, not a corner of a gym floor.

What's in the room

Five tools, each with a job.

Described by what they support. Recovery equipment is not medical treatment, and we won't tell you otherwise.

Normatec 3.0 compression

Sequential air compression through the legs. Supports circulation and gives a comfortable, passive twenty to thirty minutes after a hard session.

Sunlighten mPulse infrared sauna

Radiant heat at a lower ambient temperature than a traditional sauna. Supports warmth and relaxation; most people use ten to twenty minutes.

Higher Dose infrared PEMF mat

A heated mat combining infrared warmth with low-level pulsed electromagnetic fields. Supports a calm, warm reset between training days.

Hyperice percussion

Handheld percussive tools with a range of heads. Useful before training as part of a warm-up, and after as a general down-regulation.

Vibration platform

A stable platform used for light movement and warm-up work. Short exposures, a minute or two per position.

Somewhere quiet

The room is private and bookable. Recovery you actually do is worth more than recovery you intend to do, and a door helps.

A note on claims

Recovery equipment gets marketed with a lot of confident language about detoxification, inflammation, and metabolic effects. We describe what these tools support — circulation, warmth, relaxation, comfortable movement — and leave it there. If you have a medical condition or a specific injury, talk to a licensed healthcare provider about whether any of this is appropriate for you.
