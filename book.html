Before you come in

Common questions.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. There is no fitness prerequisite and the assessment involves no workout. Every program starts wherever you actually are.

What should I wear?

Whatever you can move comfortably in. You won't be training, so normal clothes are fine as long as you can bend and reach.

Is this a sales pitch?

It's an assessment. If coaching makes sense, Jason will say so and explain what it would look like. If it doesn't, he'll say that too.

I have an injury — should I still come?

Come and say so up front. IMS does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. If your situation needs a licensed healthcare provider first, you'll be told plainly rather than sold a package.

We don't just train you. We rebuild how you move.
